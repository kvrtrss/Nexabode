# Nexabode
**Boarding House Bed Occupancy & Payment Monitoring System**

A C#-based management system for handling room and bed occupancy, tenant records, rental billing, utility charges, and payment monitoring.

**Status:** In Development  
**Technology:** C#

## Features
- Room and bed occupancy management
- Tenant records and occupancy periods
- Monthly rent and utility billing
- Payment history and balance monitoring
- Overdue payment tracking
- Printable billing statements and reports

## Scope & Limitations
Designed for a single boarding house and one administrator account. The system does not include online payment integration, multi-property management, or hardware-based utility submetering.
